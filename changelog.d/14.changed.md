Adopt core's four introspection protocol families -- `StructureIntrospectionSupport`, `OperationalStatsIntrospectionSupport`, `DataProfileIntrospectionSupport` and `DdlExtractionIntrospectionSupport`, each with an async twin -- in place of the single `IntrospectionSupport`. The info panel reports one capability group per family instead of one Introspection row, which averaged an answered family with a declined one, and the CLI's `partitions` type gates on `supports_partition_metadata_introspection` rather than the deleted `supports_partition_info`. The introspectors also answer core's neutral object-state API -- `get_table_state`, `get_partition_state`, `list_constraint_states`, `list_schemas` and `item_exists` -- with core's state records, folding the raw `COLUMNS` answer into one cached read shared by the column listing and the partition question.
